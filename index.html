      The Kashti experience model

      See. Build.Become.

      From exposure to capability to clarity: a journey that transforms.

      1. Step 01 · Exposure

        See

        Learners encounter real environments, industries, institutions, experts and ideas.

        Learner takeawayCuriosity and context: what the world beyond the classroom actually looks like.

      2. Step 02 · Capability

        Build

        Strengthens critical thinking, communication, collaboration, leadership and problem-solving.

        Learner takeawaySkills that transfer, practised in real settings and not memorised.

      3. Step 03 · Clarity

        Become

        Helps learners explore interests, career pathways and higher-education options.

        Learner takeawayDirection: a clearer sense of where learning leads next.
